# Sensor Specifications and Acoustic Measurement Notes

## Vibration Sensor (Piezoelectric Accelerometer)
- Usable dynamic frequency response: 10 Hz to 10,000 Hz.
- Sensitivity: 100 mV/g.
- Mount: Magnetic base or stud mount onto idler bearing housing.
- Baseline roller passing frequency at 1.0 m/s: 18.2 Hz (fundamental) with harmonics at 36.4 Hz and 54.6 Hz.

## Acoustic Emission Sensor
- Frequency band: 20 kHz to 100 kHz (ultrasonic contact sensor).
- High-pass analog filtering cutoff: 15 kHz to attenuate ambient industrial motor hum.
- Coupling: Silicone acoustic couplant with consistent contact clamp force.
- Observation: Mechanical friction and localized micro-cracking emit high-frequency bursts (>30 kHz) not present in smooth roller rotation.
