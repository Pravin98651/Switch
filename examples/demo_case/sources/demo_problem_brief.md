# Conveyor Belt Early Defect Detection — Problem Brief

## Original Idea Statement
Explore whether a combination of vibration and acoustic measurements can help identify an early mechanical defect in a conveyor-belt test setup before visible tear occurs.

## Operating Context
- Benchtop conveyor loop running standard synthetic/rubber belt.
- Variable speed motorized drive with idler and drive rollers.
- Acoustic noise from motor bearings and roller contacts.

## Technical Objectives
- Identify if surface micro-defects or carcass irregularities induce transient acoustic bursts or localized vibration spikes.
- Distinguish defect signal from baseline background vibration caused by roller eccentricity and drive motor harmonics.
- Develop an experiment that can be safely run on a small benchtop rig.
