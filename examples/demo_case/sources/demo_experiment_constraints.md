# Experimental Apparatus and Boundary Constraints

## Apparatus Specification
- Benchtop test rig with 1.2 m belt loop length.
- Speed controller capability: 0.5 m/s to 2.5 m/s in 0.1 m/s increments.
- Drive motor: 24V DC brushless motor with PWM speed control.
- Artificial defect fixture: Controlled, non-destructive surface discontinuity (applied 1 mm rubber splice notch, removable test clip).

## Safety and Operational Rules
- Never modify or damage operational industrial machinery.
- Emergency stop button within immediate reach of operator.
- Sensor cables secured to prevent entanglement in rotating rollers.
- Benchtop enclosure shield in place during runs exceeding 1.5 m/s.
